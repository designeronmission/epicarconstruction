Project Overview

This 1800 square foot smart home represents the pinnacle of modern residential construction in Madurai. Designed for a tech-savvy family seeking both luxury and efficiency, this project integrates cutting-edge automation systems with sustainable building practices.

The home features a seamless blend of contemporary architecture with traditional elements, creating a living space that is both futuristic and comfortable. Every aspect of this home has been carefully planned to optimize energy efficiency, security, and convenience.

Sustainable Building Features

This project emphasizes eco-friendly construction without compromising on luxury or functionality.

  • Rainwater harvesting system with 5000L capacity
  • Solar water heating for year-round hot water supply
  • Double-glazed windows with UV protection
  • Energy-efficient LED lighting throughout
  • Low-VOC paints and sustainable flooring materials
  • Native drought-resistant landscaping with drip irrigation
